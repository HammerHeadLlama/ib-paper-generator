IB Past Paper Generator

Builds a practice paper from real past-paper questions: pick topics, paper types and marks, review it, then download the questions and the mark scheme as two PDFs.

1 Load your past papers

…or drag the folder here

Your PDFs stay on your computer: they are read by this page only and never uploaded. IB past papers are copyright of the International Baccalaureate, so they are not included here; use the copies your school gives you.

2 Settings

Topics

Weight: A bit = fewer questions from that topic, A lot = more.

Marks by paper type

Changing the total adjusts the paper type marked "rest".

Options

(1999 papers are low-quality scans, so the default starts at 2000)

Same settings + same seed = the same paper. Whole questions are picked first; stand-alone parts top up the last few marks so the total is exact.

3 Review

Remove or replace any question, or untick parts to keep only some of a question (parts it depends on stay in). Then press Fill the gaps to bring each paper type back to its marks with new questions. Removed questions never come back.

Questions, mark schemes and diagrams come from your own copies of IB past papers. This tool only records where each question is and which syllabus topics it covers.
